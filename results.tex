\subsection*{A likely chloroplast targeting SOD2 protein, with a bipartite
leader, is conserved across multiple species of symbiodinium}

In algae, the chloroplast targeting version of SOD2 includes a bipartite
leader. DeepLoc 2.1 consistently finds a SP leader XXX amino acids upstream of
PF00081, and also consistently categorizes the SP-cleaved copies of the
proteins as localized to plastid. DeepLoc employs different mechanisms (or
"heads" in their ML architecture) for classifying localization and finding
leader sequences, and was not trained on symbiodiniums. The SP cleavage point
sits XXX amino acids upstream of PF00081, suggesting a bipartite leader
architecture of XXX AAs of SP, YYY AAs of cTP, then PF00081. This class is
termed sp\_ctp\_algae, in figures and tables in this paper.

Separately, a secreted version of SOD2 with only a SP, XXX amino acids upstream
of PF00081, also exist in algae. This class is termed sp\_algae.
